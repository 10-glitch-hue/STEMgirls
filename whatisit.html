What is it? Who are we?

 Girls, United is a student-made website prototype that is meant to serve as a platform for learning and encouraging young girls and women to pursue STEM. From indepth explanations, to resourceful researches and quizzes, Girls, United facilitates learning for girls all over the world!

Did you know that, at the current growth rate, women in STEM will not achieve equal representation until 2070!! We are here to accelerate that! Nearly a quarter (23.99%) of people in 2024 believed that encouragement from a young age would help increase the number of women pursuing STEM careers (AIPRM).
